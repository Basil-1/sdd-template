# Roadmap

## Purpose

Single source of truth for what the project is aiming at and what is being worked on now. Updated as vision evolves and work completes.

The roadmap serves three roles:

1. **The current campaign** — the whole vision of the immediate plan. Increments chunk away at it, and it is inspected and revised regularly as it is built.
2. **Refined long-term decisions** — behavior known to be wanted and defined well enough to build, whose prerequisites are still being built. They become actionable when what they rely on is delivered.
3. **Speculative ambitions** — unrefined ideas, kept visible so they are on the table when choosing the direction of a next campaign.

Headings, ordering, and granularity are each project's own choice, as long as the three roles stay distinguishable.

## Rules

- Prune, don't archive. Delete delivered or abandoned topics on sight. Git history is the archive.
- A **campaign** is a multi-increment vision of the current plan. It may span several domains and may or may not carry a user-facing narrative. It decomposes into **increments** — each one a single feature branch and inspection cycle, chambered narrow enough to execute cleanly.
- Define one increment at a time. The inspection of a completed increment will often inform the next one.
- A speculative ambition graduates into a refined decision when a planning session defines it well enough to build. A refined decision joins a campaign when its prerequisites are met.
